# 🚨 CriticalPath AI — AI-Powered Emergency Response & Smart Crash Rescue System

> **From Crash Detection to Hospital Arrival — Every Second Matters.**

**CriticalPath AI** is an **AI-assisted emergency response platform** designed to reduce the time between a road accident and the arrival of appropriate medical assistance.

The system combines **vehicle telemetry, CCTV-based incident detection, manual SOS, AI-assisted severity classification, intelligent responder assignment, hospital prioritization, real-time incident coordination, and voice-based paramedic updates** into a unified emergency response workflow.

Instead of treating an accident as a simple emergency call, CriticalPath AI creates an **end-to-end digital emergency corridor** connecting:

**🚗 Vehicle → 🧠 AI Detection → 🚨 Dispatcher → 🚑 Responder → 🏥 Hospital**

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Road accidents can become fatal not only because of the initial impact, but because of delays in:

* Detecting the accident
* Confirming whether the incident is genuine
* Understanding accident severity
* Dispatching the appropriate emergency vehicle
* Selecting a suitable hospital
* Communicating patient information
* Navigating emergency responders through traffic

Traditional emergency workflows often depend heavily on manual reporting and communication.

### CriticalPath AI's Approach

CriticalPath AI transforms emergency response from a **reactive process** into a **proactive, data-driven workflow**.

> **Detect Early → Assess Intelligently → Dispatch Faster → Route Smarter → Prepare Hospitals**
